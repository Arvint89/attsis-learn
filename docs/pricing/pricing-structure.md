## Revenue Projection — Small Group of 3 Students

| Scenario | Students | Model | Revenue |
|----------|----------|-------|---------|
| Sibling family (L1 + L2) | 2 | 8-session bundle | $520 |
| 1 additional L1 student | 1 | 8-session bundle | $280 |
| 1 additional L1 student | 1 | Monthly × 2 | $296 |
| **Total · 3–4 students · 8 weeks** | 3–4 | Mixed | **$800–$1,096** |

**Deductions (first cohort):**
- Setup costs (table, chairs, cleaning): -$80
- Trial kits (LEGO + Arduino): -$210
- Consumables (8 weeks): -$60
- VSC + insurance: -$330

**Net first cohort: $120–$416 CAD**
**Second cohort onward (setup costs recovered): $800–$1,096 CAD**

> First cohort net is low — that is normal and expected. You are buying equipment, building credibility, and generating the first photos and testimonials. Cohort 2 onward is clean profit with zero setup cost.
