# Session 08 — Demo Day

## Learning objective
Students complete their project, present it to the class with technical and contextual explanation, and receive their certificate of completion.

## Format
Final 15 minutes of build time followed by structured presentations. Parents are welcome.

## Session structure
1. **Final build time** (15 min) — last iteration pass; instructor circulates with "stop coding" signal at 15 min mark
2. **Presentations** (45 min) — each student presents for 4–5 minutes

## Presentation structure (students follow this)
1. **What does it do?** — demonstrate it live
2. **How does it work?** — explain the key sensor, the key logic decision, the key output
3. **What was the hardest problem you solved?** — must be specific ("the sensor was reading noise, so I added a 10 ms delay between readings")
4. **What would version 2 look like?** — one improvement they'd make with more time

3. **Q&A from class** (10 min) — each presenter takes two questions
4. **Certificates** (5 min) — instructor presents; group photo

## Instructor notes
"What was the hardest problem you solved?" is the most important question. It teaches students to articulate technical work — a skill they will use in every job interview of their career.
