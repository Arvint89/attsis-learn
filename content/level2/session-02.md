# Session 02 — Electricity Basics

## Learning objective
Students understand voltage, current, and resistance; can read a simple schematic; and build a working LED circuit on a breadboard.

## Key concepts
- Voltage (V): electrical pressure — what pushes current
- Current (A): flow of electrons through a conductor
- Resistance (Ω): opposition to current flow; Ohm's Law V = IR
- Series vs parallel circuits: how components share voltage and current
- Reading a schematic: symbols for resistor, LED, battery, switch

## Activities
1. **Ohm's Law intro** (15 min) — water pipe analogy; worked examples: given V and R, find I
2. **Schematic reading** (10 min) — students decode three simple schematics before touching any hardware
3. **Breadboard walkthrough** (10 min) — anatomy of a breadboard; which rows connect
4. **Build an LED circuit** (25 min) — 9V battery → resistor → LED → ground; students calculate resistor value first using Ohm's Law
5. **Series vs parallel** (15 min) — add a second LED in series, then in parallel; observe brightness difference; explain why

## Materials
- 9V battery + snap connector
- Breadboard (1 per student)
- LEDs (red + green), 330Ω and 1kΩ resistors
- Multimeter (1 per pair)

## Instructor notes
Require students to calculate resistor value before handing one over. No calculation = no component. This builds the habit of thinking before building.
