# Session 01 — Engineering Thinking

## Learning objective
Students understand the engineering design process, can apply structured problem-solving to an open challenge, and know the safety rules for the course lab.

## Key concepts
- Engineering design process: Define → Research → Ideate → Prototype → Test → Iterate
- Constraints vs requirements: what must it do vs what can't it do
- Lab safety: electricity, tools, handling components
- Failure is data, not defeat

## Activities
1. **Lab safety walkthrough** (10 min) — rules, component handling, what never to connect directly to power
2. **Case study: the paper bridge** (20 min) — teams build a paper bridge to span 20 cm and hold maximum weight using only 3 sheets; debrief on design decisions made under constraints
3. **Design process poster** (15 min) — students map their bridge build to the 6 steps
4. **Course overview** (20 min) — what we build in sessions 1–8; each student writes their personal project goal
5. **Exit reflection** (10 min) — "What does a good engineer do when something fails?"

## Materials
- A4 paper (3 sheets per team), tape, small weights (coins or washers)
- Design process poster (printed, A3)

## Instructor notes
The paper bridge is deliberately unconstrained in design — observe whether students test incrementally or go straight to a final build. Debrief on this explicitly.
