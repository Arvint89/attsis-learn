# Session 02 — Parts of a Robot

## Learning objective
Students can name and describe every major component of the course robot and explain what each part does.

## Key concepts
- Body / chassis — structure and protection
- Brain / controller — where decisions happen
- Sensors — eyes and ears (touch, light, distance)
- Actuators — wheels, motors, arms
- Power — batteries, how energy flows

## Activities
1. **Component parade** (15 min) — each part laid out; students pick up, describe, guess function
2. **Build the chassis** (25 min) — guided assembly of the robot frame as a class
3. **Power it on** (10 min) — instructor connects power; students observe what happens with no program
4. **Parts quiz** (10 min) — instructor points to a component, students call out the name

## Materials
- Build kit components (sorted by type)
- Parts reference card (laminated, 1 per table)

## Instructor notes
Let students hold every part before attaching it. Physical familiarity speeds later build sessions significantly.
