# Session 07 — Build Day

## Learning objective
Students design, build, and program their own robot to perform a self-chosen task — applying all skills from sessions 1–6.

## Format
Open build session. Each pair chooses their own challenge from a provided list (or proposes their own). Instructor circulates and coaches.

## Challenge menu (students pick one)
- Line follower: robot follows a taped line on the floor
- Sumo: robot stays on a circle and pushes a lightweight object out
- Explorer: robot navigates a simple maze without touching walls
- Delivery bot: picks up and drops off an object at a marked zone

## Session structure
1. **Choose challenge + sketch plan** (10 min)
2. **Build and wire** (20 min)
3. **Code** (20 min)
4. **Test and iterate** (10 min)

## Instructor notes
Some pairs will not finish — that is fine and expected. Focus on process over product. Celebrate decisions made, not just outcomes achieved.
