# Session 01 — Meet the Machines

## Learning objective
Students understand what a robot is, where robots exist in daily life, and what parts every robot needs to work.

## Key concepts
- Definition of a robot: a machine that senses, thinks, and acts
- Real-world robots: dishwasher, traffic light, vacuum cleaner, assembly line arm
- Three core parts: sensor (input), brain (processor), actuator (output)

## Activities
1. **Robot hunt** (10 min) — name every robot you saw today before coming to class
2. **Label the robot** (20 min) — printed diagram of a simple robot; students label sensor, brain, motor
3. **Class robot unboxing** (20 min) — instructor reveals the build kit used for the course; students examine each part
4. **Exit card** (10 min) — draw one robot from real life and name its sensor + actuator

## Materials
- Printed robot diagram (1 per student)
- Build kit (1 per pair)
- Coloured markers

## Instructor notes
Keep robot examples relatable — no sci-fi robots. Focus on things students already interact with.
