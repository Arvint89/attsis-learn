# Session 03 — Move It!

## Learning objective
Students connect motors to the controller, run their first movement program, and understand how motor direction maps to wheel direction.

## Key concepts
- How a DC motor converts electricity into rotation
- Left vs right motor direction — why it matters for steering
- Forward, backward, left turn, right turn as commands

## Activities
1. **Motor demo** (10 min) — bare motor connected to battery; students observe spin direction when leads reversed
2. **Wire your wheels** (20 min) — students connect both motors to controller under instructor guidance
3. **First drive** (20 min) — run a pre-loaded "square" program; robot drives a square on the floor
4. **Challenge** (10 min) — can you make it drive back to start? Students modify forward time

## Materials
- Assembled chassis from Session 02
- DC motors + wheels
- Pre-loaded square program file

## Instructor notes
The "square" program is pre-written. Focus is on physical connection and observation, not coding yet.
